1. Introduction

Welcome to Universal Calculator ("we", "our", or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how the App collects, uses, and safeguards information when you download and use Universal Calculator from the Google Play Store.

Summary: Universal Calculator performs all calculations and unit conversions locally on your device. We do not collect, sell, or track personal identification information (such as your name, email, phone number, contacts, or location).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Universal Calculator is built with privacy-by-design principles:

  • We do not require user registration, accounts, or logins.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or media files.
  • We do not track your precise GPS location.
  • We do not upload your math formulas, loan amounts, age calculations, or notes to any external server.

3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

To provide utility and convenience, the App saves certain data strictly on your local device storage:

  • Calculation History: Past calculations and results are stored in a local SQLite database (Room) on your phone. You can clear this history anytime via the "Clear All History" button in the app.
  • App Settings & Preferences: Your selected visual theme (Light/Dark/System), angle mode (Degree/Radian), decimal precision, and haptic feedback toggles are saved locally via Android Preferences.
  • Cached Currency Rates: Currency exchange rates are cached locally so conversions remain functional when offline.

This data never leaves your device and is erased if you clear app storage or uninstall the application.

4. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only the minimum Android permissions strictly required for its features:

Permission Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Used to download updated public currency exchange rates and to serve advertisements via Google AdMob.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Checks network availability prior to refreshing currency rates to prevent failed network requests.
android.permission.VIBRATE Enables tactile haptic feedback when pressing keypad buttons (can be disabled in Settings).

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs to support its development and free availability:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC):
    The App displays banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may process non-personally identifiable diagnostic data and device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID / GAID) in accordance with Google Play policies to serve relevant ads and detect fraud.
    Review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy
    Learn about how Google manages data in advertising products: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Public Currency API:
    When updating currency exchange rates, the App issues anonymous HTTPS requests to public exchange rate feeds. No personal or device data is sent in these requests.

6. User Privacy Controls (Opt-Out)

You have complete control over advertising and data on your device:

  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android devices (Settings → Google → Ads), you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it entirely to opt out of personalized advertisements.
  • Clear Calculation History: You can wipe all calculation history directly inside the History tab with a single tap.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling the application deletes all locally stored data, history, and preferences immediately.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Universal Calculator is a general utility tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at sujjat.info21@gmail.com and we will take immediate corrective action.

8. Data Security

Because all user calculations remain localized on your device's sandboxed storage, your calculations and financial estimates are secure and isolated from third-party interception. All external network communication (e.g., currency rate checks) is performed strictly over secure TLS/HTTPS connections.
